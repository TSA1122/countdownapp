**Remainder: Your Countdown Companion**

**About Remainder:**
Remainder is a sleek and intuitive countdown app designed to keep you connected with the world's most anticipated events. Whether it's global celebrations, country-specific festivals, or personal milestones, Remainder ensures you never miss an important date.

**Key Features:**

1. **Predefined Countdowns:**
   - **Global Events:** Stay updated on major worldwide events like New Year’s Eve, FIFA World Cup, and the Olympics.
   - **Country-Specific:** Discover and track festivals and holidays relevant to your location or interests, such as Diwali in India or Christmas in the U.S.

2. **Favourites Management:**
   - **Add favourites:** Pin your most anticipated events to the dashboard for quick access.
   - **Remove favourites:** Easily manage your list to keep it clutter-free.

3. **Interactive Dashboard:**
   - **Clear Overview:** The main page displays all favourited countdowns with a clean interface.
   - **Countdown Cards:** Each card shows the remaining time in years, days, hours, and minutes, enhanced with engaging visual elements like icons or progress bars.

4. **Push Notifications & Reminders:**
   - Stay informed with timely alerts as events approach, ensuring you never miss a moment.

**Included Features:**
- **Free Version:** Includes basic features with **5** favorite countdown slots.
- **Pro Upgrade:** Unlock advanced features, unlimited favourites, and premium customization options through a subscription model.

**Future Updates & Roadmap:**

1. **Widget Support:** Widgets for easy access on home screens.
2. **Custom Event Creation:** Users can create unique events beyond predefined lists.
3. **Shared Countdowns:** Ability to share countdowns with friends and family.

**Conclusion:**
Remainder is more than just a countdown app; it's your go-to tool for staying connected with what matters most. Whether it's global happenings or personal milestones, Remainder ensures you're always in the know. Join thousands of users who trust Remainder to keep their lives on track with style and ease.
